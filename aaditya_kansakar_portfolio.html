01 / BACKGROUND

A little about me.

I am a web developer and creative designer passionate about building thoughtful digital products. Rather than relying on rigid templates or exaggerated titles, I believe the best work comes from understanding fundamentals, caring about typography, and obsessing over how things look and feel in real browsers.

My work spans across the bridge between engineering and creative direction: from developing responsive layouts with clean HTML, CSS, and modern JavaScript to crafting user interfaces in Figma, producing digital graphic assets, and editing short-form video content.

I approach development through the lens of curiosity—learning by building, breaking things down to understand how they work under the hood, and continuously polishing every detail.

Core Mindset
“Learn. Build. Break. Improve. Repeat.”

A commitment to honest progress, steady problem solving, and hands-on craftsmanship.
